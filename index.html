Reasoning Computation · research programme

How do language models compute — and what happens when an intermediate step goes wrong?

Adya Srivastava. Companion programme to Retrieval vs Computation.

Benchmarks tell you whether a model got the answer right. They do not tell you how the computation was performed, or why an error sometimes propagates and sometimes does not. This site is the interactive map of the RC programme: first the paper-development protocol, then the programme, then Direction 1.

Why this is hard. A correct final answer can come from systematic computation, retrieval, templates, or shortcuts. A wrong answer does not reveal where the computation broke.

What this programme adds. A causally tested account of intermediate computational state — representation, updating, failure, recovery — starting with a sharply bounded first study.
