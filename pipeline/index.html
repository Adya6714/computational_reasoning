Layer 01 · Standard operating procedure

Research pipeline

You should not begin by opening a document and writing the Introduction. First establish what you are investigating, why it matters, what is missing, what you intend to demonstrate, and how you will know your conclusions are valid.

Think of research-paper development as a sequence of gates. Each stage produces a concrete artifact that you verify before moving to the next.
